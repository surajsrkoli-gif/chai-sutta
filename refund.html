Refund & reward reversal policy.

How Chai Sutta handles offer reversals, third-party chargebacks, incorrect reward attribution and withdrawal-related issues.

Last updated: [add date before publishing] Applies to: Chai Sutta Android app & website

1. About this policy

This policy explains how Chai Sutta approaches refunds and reward reversals. It also clarifies the difference between purchases, virtual rewards, offer completions and withdrawal requests.

2. Chai Sutta does not currently sell Chillar

Chai Sutta does not currently sell Chillar or any other virtual currency. Chillar is earned by completing eligible activities within the application. Because there is no purchase of Chillar, the platform does not treat earned Chillar as a conventional product purchase.

3. Purchases

If the application does not offer paid products at the time you are reading this policy, there is nothing to refund in the traditional sense. If paid products or purchases are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated before that feature goes live.

4. Virtual rewards

Chillar and other reward balances are virtual. They may be credited, held pending, rejected or reversed depending on the outcome of verification by Chai Sutta and its third-party partners. Virtual rewards are not equivalent to money held in a bank account.

5. Offer completion and reversals

When you complete an offer, survey or rewarded advertisement, the applicable provider may later report that the completion was not valid. In these cases:

  • a reward that has not yet been credited may simply not be credited;
  • a reward that has already been credited may be reversed;
  • a pending reward may be cancelled without being credited.

6. Third-party chargebacks

If a provider or advertiser reports a chargeback, correction or reversal for an offer that you participated in, Chai Sutta may reverse the corresponding Chillar that was previously credited to your account. Reversals may also affect any pending activity tied to the same offer.

7. Incorrect reward attribution

If a reward is credited to the wrong account, or with an incorrect amount, Chai Sutta may correct the credit or debit. Corrections are made to maintain accurate reward records for all users.

8. Failed or reversed transactions

If a withdrawal or reward transaction fails or is reversed for a technical or provider-related reason, we may restore the corresponding Chillar to your wallet, or otherwise adjust the balance to reflect the correct state.

9. Withdrawal-related issues

Withdrawal requests are subject to eligibility, verification and platform rules. If a withdrawal request cannot be completed, we will inform you where possible, and the affected Chillar may be returned to your wallet or kept pending while the issue is investigated.

10. What is not covered

This policy does not create any right to a refund for Chillar that has been earned and legitimately used. It also does not override any mandatory rights you may have under applicable law.

11. Contact

If you believe a reversal was applied incorrectly, please contact support@chaisutta.app with your User ID and the relevant offer or transaction ID. You can also use the Report an issue page.

This policy must be reviewed again if the platform introduces paid products, Chillar purchases, or other transaction types that change the nature of refunds.
