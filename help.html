Support

Chai Sutta Help Centre.

Browse help articles by category. If you can't find what you need, you can contact support or report an issue.

Account & Login

Sign-in, profile and account basics.

Confirm you are using the mobile number registered with your Chai Sutta account, check that you have network connectivity, and request a fresh OTP if needed. If the problem persists, contact support with your User ID.

Open the app, go to your account screen and edit the fields that are marked as editable. Some fields (such as the registered mobile number) may not be changeable directly and may require contacting support.

Earning & Offers

Finding and completing activities.

Offer visibility depends on your region, device, provider, campaign and eligibility. Try refreshing the list, checking your connection, and revisiting later — new offers are added as campaigns go live.

Many third-party activities require a verification period before the reward is confirmed. If a reward stays pending significantly longer than usual, please report it with the offer ID and your User ID.

Make sure you completed the offer's requirements exactly. Some offers require reaching a certain level, completing a purchase or finishing a survey in one sitting. If you believe you met the requirements, please report the issue.

Rejections usually mean the provider could not verify completion, or the activity did not meet the offer's requirements. You can submit a report with the offer ID if you believe the rejection was incorrect.

Chillar & Wallet

Balance, history and transaction records.

Balances update as activities are verified. Pending items are not counted as available Chillar. If you believe your balance is wrong after checking your transaction history, please contact support.

Open the Wallet section of the app. Every credit, reversal and withdrawal is listed with a status and timestamp.

Withdrawals

Requesting a payout from your wallet.

Withdrawals can take time depending on region and provider. Check the status in your wallet history. If it stays in a pending state beyond the expected window shown in the app, please report the issue.

Minimum thresholds and available withdrawal methods are shown in the app at the time of your request. They can vary by region and may change over time.

Referrals

Inviting friends and qualifying rewards.

Referrals only qualify when the invited user meets the program's requirements (for example, completing a first eligible activity). Self-referrals and duplicate accounts do not qualify.

Open the Refer & Earn section in the app to see your code and share it with friends.

Technical Problems

App crashes, installs and updates.

Force-close and reopen the app, make sure you're on the latest version, and try clearing the app cache from Android settings. If the crash persists, report the issue with your device model and Android version.

Confirm you have enough free storage, that the download completed fully, and that your Android version meets the minimum requirement. Only install the APK from the official Chai Sutta website.

Security

Protecting your account.

Never share your OTP, password or account credentials with anyone — including people claiming to be from Chai Sutta support. Our team will never ask you for these.

If you notice unfamiliar activity in your wallet or account, immediately contact support and change any credentials you believe may be compromised.

Account Restrictions

Understanding and appealing restrictions.

Account restrictions are usually the result of automated anti-fraud checks or violations of our Terms of Service. If you believe the restriction was applied in error, please contact support with your User ID.

Send an email to support@chaisutta.app with your User ID, the reason shown in the app, and any supporting context. Appeals are reviewed case by case.
