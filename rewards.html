Understand Chillar.

Chillar is Chai Sutta's virtual in-app reward currency. This page explains how it works, how it can be earned, and what it is — and is not.

What is Chillar?

A virtual in-app reward currency

Chillar is the internal reward currency used within the Chai Sutta application. You earn it by completing eligible activities, and you can use your available balance according to the platform's current wallet and withdrawal rules.

Chillar is not cryptocurrency, not real money, not a bank balance, not a security or investment, and not a guaranteed source of income.

Reward status

What your Chillar status means

Chillar from an activity may pass through several states before it becomes available in your wallet.

Pending

Pending

The activity has been recorded but is awaiting verification. Third-party activities commonly remain pending for a period before conversion.

Processing

Processing

The provider has confirmed the activity and our systems are converting it into Chillar for your wallet.

Completed

Completed

The Chillar has been credited to your balance and appears in your wallet and transaction history.

Rejected

Rejected

The provider or our verification systems determined the activity did not meet the offer's requirements. No Chillar is issued.

Reversed

Reversed

A previously credited reward was reversed — for example due to a chargeback, provider correction or detected policy violation.

Verification

Third-party activities may require verification by the provider before Chillar becomes available. Verification timing is outside Chai Sutta's direct control.

Reward transparency. Actual reward amounts vary by activity, provider, eligibility and campaign availability. Chai Sutta does not display fixed earning amounts because they are not guaranteed.
Important

Chillar is not cryptocurrency

Chillar is an internal virtual reward currency used within Chai Sutta and should not be treated as cryptocurrency, an investment or a financial product. It has no value outside the platform and cannot be traded, transferred or exchanged on any external market.
