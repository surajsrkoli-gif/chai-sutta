Privacy Policy.

This policy describes how Chai Sutta handles information in connection with the Chai Sutta Android application and this website.

Last updated: [add date before publishing] Applies to: Chai Sutta Android app & website

1. Introduction

Chai Sutta ("we", "us", "our") operates an Android rewards application and an associated website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you. By using the Chai Sutta application or website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: name or display name, email address, and account identifier.
  • Authentication information: mobile number and OTP verification data used to sign in.
  • Profile information: optional profile details you choose to provide.
  • App activity: activity within the app such as screens viewed, features used and interactions with offers.
  • Reward / transaction history: Chillar credits, debits, reversals and withdrawal requests.
  • Referral information: referral codes used and referral relationships, where the referral program is active.
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, app version and general device identifiers.
  • IP address: used for security, fraud prevention, and to determine approximate region.
  • Approximate location: where required by third-party reward providers for offer eligibility.
  • Advertising / offer interaction data: events related to offers, surveys and rewarded advertisements, including their status.
  • Support communications: information you provide when you contact support or report an issue.

We do not intentionally collect more information than is reasonably required to operate the rewards service and to keep it secure.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above for purposes including:

  • creating and authenticating your account;
  • providing the rewards service, including crediting eligible Chillar;
  • preventing fraud, abuse and manipulation of the reward system;
  • processing offerwall, survey and advertising events received from third-party providers;
  • maintaining accurate transaction and reward records;
  • responding to support requests and issue reports;
  • improving the app, its features and reliability;
  • maintaining the security and integrity of the service.

4. Third-Party Providers

Some reward activities — including offerwalls, surveys and rewarded advertisements — are provided or tracked by third-party services. When you interact with those activities, the applicable provider may collect information directly under its own privacy policy.

Chai Sutta does not control the privacy practices of third-party providers. Their own terms and privacy policies apply to the data they process. You should review those policies before interacting with a third-party activity.

5. Firebase / Cloud Infrastructure

Chai Sutta uses Firebase and related Google Cloud services for account authentication, database and backend functionality. These services may process information required to provide these functions, including identifiers, device information and app activity.

6. Advertising

If advertising is integrated into the application, information such as device identifiers and ad interaction data may be processed to deliver and measure advertisements. Where advertising is not yet integrated, this section does not apply.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the service, to comply with legal obligations, to resolve disputes and to enforce our agreements. Specific retention periods depend on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

Retention periods have not been finalized and must be reviewed by the project owner before publication.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights relating to your personal information, such as the right to request access, correction or deletion. The exact rights available to you depend on the applicable law in your location. To exercise a right, please contact us using the details below.

10. Children's Privacy

The Chai Sutta application is not directed to children. The service is intended for users who meet the minimum age required to enter into a contract in their jurisdiction, and any additional age requirement that may be required to participate in reward activities. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact: support@chaisutta.app.

This Privacy Policy is provided as a starting point and must be reviewed and finalized by the project owner, and where appropriate by qualified legal counsel, before being relied upon in production.
