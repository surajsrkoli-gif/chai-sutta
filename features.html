Product

Everything you need to discover rewards.

Chai Sutta brings eligible reward opportunities into one lightweight Android app — offers, surveys, tasks and rewarded ads, all tracked from a single Chillar wallet.

Offerwalls

Discover eligible offers from supported reward partners. Availability depends on your region, device and current campaigns.

Surveys

Participate in available surveys when you match the required eligibility criteria. Not every survey is available to every user.

Daily Tasks

Complete available tasks and activities to earn Chillar. Fresh opportunities may appear as providers publish new campaigns.

Rewarded Ads

Watch eligible rewarded advertisements when available. Ad inventory changes throughout the day and by region.

Refer & Earn

Invite friends using your referral code and participate in the referral program, subject to eligibility and program rules.

Chillar Wallet

Track your available Chillar and transaction history. Balances update as activity is verified by our reward system.

Offer History

Review your reward activity and transaction records. Each entry shows status, source and any pending verification.

Secure Account

Account and reward operations use server-side validation and protected backend systems.

Anti-Fraud

Reward activity is checked using server-side validation and anti-abuse mechanisms before Chillar is credited.

Important: Availability of offers, surveys, tasks, rewarded ads and other activities varies by country, region, device, provider, user eligibility, advertiser, campaign and platform configuration. Chai Sutta does not guarantee that any specific activity will always be available.
