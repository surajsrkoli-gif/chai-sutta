How Chai Sutta works.

Five simple steps — from creating your account to using the Chillar you've earned. No complicated setup.

01

Create your account

Create a Chai Sutta account and complete the required account setup. You'll need a mobile number for verification and to accept our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

02

Explore opportunities

Browse eligible offers, surveys, tasks, rewarded ads and other available activities. What you see depends on your region, device, eligibility and the campaigns currently active with our reward partners.

03

Complete an activity

Follow the instructions provided by the applicable activity or third-party provider. Some activities require you to reach a certain level, complete a purchase, or finish a survey in one sitting.

04

Receive Chillar

Eligible completed activities are processed through the platform's reward system after required verification. Verification can take time and the resulting Chillar may appear as pending in your wallet until confirmed.

05

Use available rewards

Eligible Chillar can be used according to the current wallet and withdrawal rules of the platform. Withdrawal options and minimum thresholds may vary by region.

Please read carefully. Completing an activity does not automatically guarantee a reward unless the applicable offer explicitly says so. Some activities require verification, rewards may remain pending for a period, and third-party providers may reject or reverse conversions. Reward availability varies and is not controlled solely by Chai Sutta.
