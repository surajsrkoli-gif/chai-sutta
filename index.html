Why Chai Sutta

Small breaks.
Genuine rewards.

Everything in the app is designed to be finished in under two minutes — perfect for that one cup of chai.

Daily chai bonus

Open the app once a day, tap your cup and collect a bonus. Streaks grow your multiplier up to 3× — miss a day and it resets, so it stays interesting.

Watch & earn

A short feed of clips and quick tasks. Each one credits coins instantly to your balance — no forms, no spam, no forced downloads.

Instant UPI payouts

Link your UPI ID once and withdraw any time you cross the minimum. Most payouts land in your account within minutes, not days.

Spin, scratch, win

Gamified reward moments keep the experience fun. Spin the wheel or scratch a card every few hours for bonus coins and surprise drops.

Refer & grow

Share your invite code. When a friend joins and completes their first task, you both get a bonus — plus a small cut of their earnings for 30 days.

Light & fast

Under 12 MB installed, optimised for Android 6.0 and up. Runs smoothly on budget phones and low-bandwidth connections.

Everything you might ask

UPI withdrawals are typically processed within a few minutes. During peak hours or bank downtime it can take up to 24 hours. You can track the status of every request from the Wallet screen.
