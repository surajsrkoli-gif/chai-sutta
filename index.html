Why Chai Sutta

Small breaks.
Real activities.

Everything in the app is designed to be finished in under two minutes — perfect for that one cup of chai.

Offerwalls

Discover eligible offers from supported reward partners. Availability depends on your region, device and current campaigns.

Surveys

Participate in available surveys when you match the required eligibility criteria. Not every survey is available to every user.

Daily tasks

Complete available tasks and activities to earn Chillar. Fresh opportunities may appear as providers publish new campaigns.

Rewarded ads

Watch eligible rewarded advertisements when available. Ad inventory changes throughout the day and by region.

Refer & earn

Invite friends using your referral code and participate in the referral program, subject to eligibility and program rules.

Chillar wallet

Track your available Chillar and transaction history. Balances update as activity is verified by our reward system.
