Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about Chai Sutta, Chillar, offers, rewards and accounts. Still stuck? Visit the Help Centre.

Chai Sutta is an Android rewards application that lets eligible users discover and participate in activities such as offerwalls, surveys, rewarded advertisements, daily tasks and referrals. Completed eligible activities are processed through our reward system and may credit Chillar to your wallet after verification.

Chillar is Chai Sutta's in-app virtual reward currency. It is used to track rewards earned within the application and can be used according to the platform's current wallet and withdrawal rules. Chillar is not cryptocurrency, not real money, and not a financial product.

You can earn Chillar by completing eligible activities such as offerwall offers, surveys you qualify for, daily tasks, rewarded advertisements and, when available, referrals and other supported activities. Availability depends on your region, device, eligibility and current campaigns.

Offers, surveys and ads are supplied by third-party providers and advertisers. Availability varies by country, region, device, provider, advertiser campaign and platform configuration. Some offers may also be restricted by age or eligibility criteria.

Third-party activities often require verification before the reward is confirmed. During this period the reward may appear as pending in your wallet. The length of the pending period depends on the provider and activity type.

A reward may be rejected if the activity did not meet the offer's requirements, if the provider's systems could not verify completion, if instructions were not followed, or if our anti-fraud systems detected suspicious behaviour. Rejections are decided based on provider feedback and platform verification.

Yes. In some situations a reward may be reversed after it was credited — for example if the provider later reports a chargeback, correction or policy violation, or if our anti-fraud review finds the activity invalid. Reversed rewards are removed from your Chillar balance.

You can invite friends using your referral code. When an invited friend registers and meets the qualifying conditions described in the referral program, you may receive a referral reward according to the program rules. Referral rules, qualifying conditions and rewards may change. Self-referrals and fake accounts are not permitted.

Withdrawal options, minimum thresholds and processing times depend on your region and the rules shown in the app at the time of the request. Withdrawal availability may also depend on account status, verification status and applicable provider requirements.

Reward amounts depend on the specific activity, the third-party provider, your eligibility, the advertiser's campaign rules and the region. Two users may see different offers and different reward values for the same category of activity.
